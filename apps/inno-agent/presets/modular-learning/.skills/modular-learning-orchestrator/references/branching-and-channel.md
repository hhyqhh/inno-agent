# Branching And Channel Dialogue

## Checkpoint

At a substantial stopping point, preserve `domain + block + unresolved question + stage + choices`. Show the learner a concise choice: continue the question, see another use, enter application, inspect a related connection, or pause.

For WeChat and Feishu, one message answers one immediate question or creates one learner action. Do not send a sequence that assumes the learner read the next message. When the learner returns, recap the block and unresolved question in at most two lines.

Only after explicit opt-in and confirmation of channel, target, timezone, window, frequency cap, missed-reply behavior, and stop command may the agent call `create_scheduled_job`. Silence never justifies increased frequency.
